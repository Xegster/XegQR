Common problems

Scanning isn't available in my browser

Reading codes in a browser needs a feature called BarcodeDetector, which Chrome and Edge have but Firefox and Safari don't yet. Creating, styling and saving codes works in every browser. To scan on any device, use the Android app.

The camera doesn't start
  • Allow camera access when asked. If you said no, switch it back on in your device's app settings, or in your browser's site settings for qr.xegster.com.au.
  • Close any other app that is using the camera, then reopen the Scan screen.
  • On the web, the page must be opened over https, and some in-app browsers (inside social apps) block the camera. Open the link in your normal browser.
A code I made won't scan
  • Keep strong contrast: dark code on a light background. Very pale or very similar colours are hard to read.
  • Raise the error-correction level (Settings → Defaults, or in the style options) if you added a logo, then keep the logo small.
  • Shorter content makes a simpler code that scans from further away. Long text produces a dense code.
  • Print it large enough, and test it with a scanner before you share it.
Saving to my photos fails

On Android, XegQR asks for photo-library permission the first time you save. If you declined, allow it under your device's app settings for XegQR, then try again. On the web, saving downloads a file; check your browser's downloads folder.

My saved codes are gone, or aren't on my other device

XegQR has no account and no cloud, so saved codes live only on the device (or browser) where you made them. They are removed if you uninstall the app or clear the site's data, and they don't move between devices. Keep a screenshot or an exported image of anything important.

The home-screen tile doesn't appear (Android)

Long-press an empty spot on your home screen, choose Widgets, then find XegQR. To change a tile's icon or the code it opens, long-press it and tap its edit option (Android 12 and later). Not every launcher lets an app add a tile itself.

Delete your data

XegQR has no accounts, and we hold none of your data, so there is nothing for us to delete and no account to close. Everything is on your device, and you can remove it yourself:

  • One code: open Saved and delete it.
  • Saved logo images: Settings → Image cache → Remove all saved images.
  • Everything: uninstall the app on Android, or clear the site data for qr.xegster.com.au in your browser's settings.
